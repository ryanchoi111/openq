OpenQ Support

OpenQ support can help with account access, property records, renter inquiries, Gmail lead capture, calendar booking, profile uploads, and account deletion.

Contact

Email support@openq.app. Include the email address on your OpenQ account and a short description of the issue.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account in the app from the profile or home screen. If you cannot access the app, email support from the account email address and request deletion.

Google Connection Help

If Gmail or Calendar stops syncing, disconnect and reconnect Google from the agent profile screen. If the issue continues, contact support with the approximate time the sync failed.
