OpenQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

OpenQ helps real estate agents manage properties, renter inquiries, tour requests, calendar bookings, and follow-up. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We collect account details such as name, email address, phone number, profile photo, user role, and authentication identifiers. Agents may add property records, listing details, booking settings, documents, and calendar availability. Renters and guests may provide inquiry details, tour preferences, contact information, waitlist activity, booking answers, and application-related documents.

Connected Google Services

When an agent connects Google, OpenQ may access Gmail messages needed to identify renter leads and Google Calendar data needed to show availability or create booking events. We use this data only to provide the requested lead capture and scheduling features.

How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts, operate property and booking workflows, import and organize renter inquiries, send relevant emails, provide customer support, maintain service reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information with service providers that host the app, store files, send email, provide authentication, or process connected calendar and email workflows. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service.

Data Retention And Deletion

We retain information while an account is active or as needed to provide the service. Users can request deletion in the app from account settings. Deletion removes the account and associated user-owned records unless we are required to keep limited information for legal, security, or fraud prevention reasons.

Security

We use access controls, authentication, encrypted transport, and managed infrastructure to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact support@openq.app.
