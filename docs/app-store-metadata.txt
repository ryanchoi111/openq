Description

OpenQ helps real estate agents and property managers keep property tours, renter inquiries, and follow-up organized in one mobile workspace.

Create property records, run tour queues from QR check-ins, capture renter inquiries from Gmail, review property-specific lead activity, schedule bookings with calendar availability, upload profile and application documents, and follow up with prospective renters from the app.

For renters and guests, OpenQ supports quick QR-based tour check-in, live waitlist status, and tour history without adding friction to the showing.
